A thin square plate rings in standing waves. Each mode has lines where the plate never moves. Driven at one of its resonances, the plate shakes hard everywhere else, sand hops off the moving regions, and it comes to rest on those nodal lines. Between resonances the plate barely moves and the sand stays where it is. Ernst Chladni showed these figures with a violin bow in 1787.
